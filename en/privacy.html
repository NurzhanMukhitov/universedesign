What is collected

The site has no forms: we never ask for a name, phone number or email, and there are no user accounts. Everything collected is anonymous visit statistics gathered through Yandex.Metrica.

It covers: IP address, browser type and language, operating system, screen resolution, page address and referrer, time and duration of the visit, and on-page behaviour — cursor movement, clicks and scrolling.

Session recordings

Metrica's Session Replay is enabled: it records how a visitor moves through a page. Recordings are stored by Yandex and used only to find where the site is inconvenient. Since the site has no input fields, no typed text is ever captured — there is nothing to type.

Why it is needed

To understand which works people look at, where visitors come from and what gets in their way. The data is not used for advertising and is not matched against individual people.

Who else receives the data

Data is processed by Yandex LLC as the statistics provider — Yandex terms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so page requests appear in GitHub's service logs — their policy. No data is shared with anyone else and none of it is sold.

What the site stores in your browser

Besides Yandex.Metrica's own files, the site keeps three technical entries. They exist purely to run the interface, hold no personal data and are never sent anywhere:

  • cube_session_started — plays the cube intro once per visit instead of on every page
  • force_chaotic_cube — which state the cube starts in
  • justLeftIndex — whether to open the menu when you come back from a section

If your browser blocks storage, the site keeps working.

How to opt out

Install the Yandex.Metrica opt-out extension, turn on "Do Not Track" in your browser, or block third-party cookies in settings. The site stays fully functional either way.
