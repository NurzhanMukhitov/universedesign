REFRACTION — light installation at the NUR International Media Art Festival in Kazan

RU/EN

REFRACTION

NUR International Media Art Festival, Kazan

Installation by the media.tribe art collective with the support of UNIVERSE design studio.

REFRACTION is an experiment with perception, where light and form lose their usual outlines. We hide the light source and leave it moving and distorted. The viewer no longer sees clear objects, only their reflections, refracted through physical and psychological filters.

The installation makes you think: what do we actually see — the true world, or just a distorted version of it? It invites us to perceive reality as a continuous process of change, where the very essence lies in its instability.
